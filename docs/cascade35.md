# Heated beta-plane experiment at 35°N

Requested November 11, 2026. A new 100-Earth-day run moves the heated, initially random-eddy experiment from 55°N to 35°N. Both latitudes lie within the approximately 20–60° midlatitude belt discussed in [Duer et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07255). Latitude selection is not a prescription for a Ferrel cell. This is a shallow-channel mechanism experiment, not a reproduction of Jupiter's deep overturning.

The 6000 × 6000 × 120 km channel, 256 × 256 × 64 grid, random eddy realization, 1200-km seed wavelength, thermal perturbations, 162 W/m² bottom heating, dry anelastic physics, stable cap, thermal controller, boundary conditions and diffusivities are retained. The channel spans approximately 32.6–37.4°N in the local mapping. No initial jet or imposed shear is added.

At 35°N, `f0 = 2.017268326646629e-4 s^-1` and `beta = 4.02976240665303e-12 m^-1 s^-1`. Relative to 55°N, f decreases and beta increases. To retain the same dimensional initial velocity (about 5.5022 m/s RMS), the initializer's Rossby-number input changes from 0.1 to 0.14281480067421148. This compensates its f-dependent speed normalization; it does not inject a stronger initial flow. Saved initial velocity, temperature profile, tracer and vorticity are checked against the completed f-plane reference within `rtol=1e-12, atol=1e-13`. The pressure estimate uses the new local f0 and retains beta/nonlinear adjustment.

The dynamical source matches the previous campaign after removing only added read-only output instructions. New output includes zonal-mean velocity, temperature and tracer, centered resolved momentum/heat/tracer covariances, native meridional and vertical mass fluxes, and the corresponding mass streamfunction with continuity/reconstruction residuals. These enable later overturning screening. They are not the native closed momentum-budget diagnostics of the separate Ferrel pilot; a circulation image alone will not establish eddy-driven Ferrel cells.

A GPU gate checks the unchanged dynamical source, initializer parameters, full-state checkpoint/restart agreement including new zonal diagnostics, one day at production resolution, cap stability, thermal accounting, tracer conservation, finite new outputs, initial matching and throughput. Ten dependent ten-day jobs follow only after a passing gate. Every segment preserves its final full checkpoint. New source and output directories isolate this experiment from queued or active runs.

The comparison renderer waits for both 35°N and 55°N beta-plane day-100 jobs. It prepares three-depth vorticity-over-tracer movies with all 801 saved times, plus zonal-velocity time-versus-y diagrams and zonal kinetic-energy fractions. Both latitude movies use the same 55°N reference f for color normalization, so equal colors mean equal dimensional vorticity. The latitude labels and reference normalization remain explicit. Rendering, diagnostics and validation stay on Unity; finished media will be published to the [GitHub gallery](https://npizzo.github.io/jupiter-ffr-breeze/#beta35).

## Longer integrations and restarts

Yes: the full checkpoint is the continuation state. It includes the dynamical fields and saved timestep/controller callback state. The three-depth image-plane outputs are insufficient to restart. The completed heated f-plane run retains a 231-MB final checkpoint in `data/cascade100-v1/cascade100_f_heated/segment_10/checkpoints` (verified November 11). The beta-plane campaigns retain final checkpoints in the same way, separately for each segment. The day-100 files will not be overwritten by a new extension directory.

A continuation should preserve the same grid, latitude, physics and forcing, increase the absolute stop time, and restore the full checkpoint into a new output directory. Test the restart and verify source/configuration compatibility before extending. Changing latitude by loading a different case's checkpoint would be a separate perturbation experiment, not the matched-from-day-zero comparison submitted here.

For jets, a useful next target is 300 total Earth days, followed by 500 if jet energy, spacing or amplitude is still drifting and the physical/numerical checks remain acceptable. These are proposed observing windows, not predicted equilibration times. No additional day-100-to-300 production chain is submitted by this request. The existing launcher intentionally stops at day 100; an extension launcher must explicitly continue from the retained checkpoint rather than overwrite the original segments or merely restart from a plotted snapshot.

Longer duration cannot compensate for a domain that accommodates too few jet widths, underresolved convection, or circulation controlled by walls/cap/sponge. Evaluate zonal-flow histories, Reynolds stresses, scale-transfer diagnostics and time-block statistics before interpreting a mature jet or a Ferrel-like response. Deep spherical closure remains outside this 120-km box; [Duer et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023AV000908) emphasize the importance of vertical momentum transport and boundary conditions.
