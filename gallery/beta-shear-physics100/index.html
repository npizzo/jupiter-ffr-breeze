What the pair shows

Both runs reached t = 100. The early boundary disturbance subsided: final peak Mach numbers are 0.203 with the jet and 0.215 without it. The final maps retain several structures of both signs. This modest jet changes the late-time sign statistics slightly; it does not establish a reversal to an anticyclonic LSV, or a persistent GRS-like vortex.

The table reports the fraction of plane-integrated squared vertical vorticity carried by negative vorticity, averaged over t = 75–100. It is an anticyclonic enstrophy share, not vortex area or the strength of one identified vortex. The nonzonal measure first removes the instantaneous x-mean vorticity.

The upper-plane total-share difference is 2.9 percentage points across the whole plane, and about 1.25 points after excluding |y/H| ≥ 3.5. Boundary and zonal contributions therefore matter. Neither these statistics nor the movie demonstrate an inverse energy cascade.

Fidelity at this stage

These are coarse exploratory runs with one shared initial realization. The accumulated energy deficit at t = 100 is approximately 20% of the cumulative bottom heat input in both cases. The maximum relative drift of the horizontally averaged temperature profile is 4.56% with the jet and 3.62% without it. These limitations prevent a quantitative claim about vortex maintenance or a converged sign-selection transition.

The final horizontally averaged cap remains stably stratified. The early maximum sampled Mach number was 0.539 with the jet and 0.511 without; outside the designated top/wall region the maxima were 0.373 and 0.361. Passing the safety guards is not a convergence test. The duration is about 5.6 initial eddy-turnover times.

Configuration and how to read the colors

Dry compressible Breeze, ideal gas (R = 1, cp = 2.5), gravity 12; f₀ = 5.54328, β = 0.00321169, with the horizontal rotation component retained. The channel is periodic in x, with impermeable, stress-free north/south walls and top/bottom. Both cases start with the same mixed-sign eddy velocity field, RMS 0.1, and receive bottom heat flux 0.03125. The jet amplitude parameter is 0.015625, ramped vertically through z/H = 0.82–0.96, with momentum relaxation time 200. Mean-only cap thermal restoration is retained; there is no sponge. WENO5 advection and the native Lilly closure are used.

Vorticity is total ζ/f₀, including the jet: red/positive is cyclonic, blue/negative is anticyclonic in this Northern Hemisphere setup (x east, y north, z up). Symmetric limits are fixed per depth across both cases and every frame, encompassing all saved extrema; an asinh mapping with width 0.01 retains weak structures.

Tracer colors use the fixed contrast mapping asinh[(C − 0.1)/0.005], with absolute concentration tick labels and bounds 0–1. The saved concentrations span 0.0344–0.1728; no frame-by-frame rescaling is used. Horizontal panels are stretched for readability; the actual domain is twice as wide in y as in x. Time is nondimensional, not Earth days or physical seconds.
