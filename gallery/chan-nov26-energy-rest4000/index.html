Spectral concentration alone does not demonstrate inverse energy transfer.

The thermal state is still evolving: maximum relative mean-temperature-profile departure from the original t=0 is 91.8%; minimum cap-mean N² is -0.494. Endpoint peak Mach is 0.734. Total-energy ledger residual at the endpoint is -0.000784% of gross bottom heating over the t=2050–4000 restart segment. Completion does not establish thermal equilibrium or LSV saturation.

At the endpoint, the full three-dimensional velocity field has 0.426% of its density-weighted kinetic energy at horizontal wavelengths ≤4 grid cells, and 2.25% at wavelengths ≤8 cells. These energy fractions do not establish vorticity/enstrophy convergence, vertical resolution, or an inverse flux.

Final tracer RMS variations at the three depths are 9.35e-05, 8.64e-05, and 2.33e-04. Near-zero or Float32-scale variations should be read as homogenization, not a visualization failure.
