What is changed?

Both cases are unforced, start from identical saved initial velocities, and use the same 512² periodic 2π × 2π domain, f=1, beta=0, ν=0.0001 and timestep 0.0025. There is no drag. The left panel adds DA=0.0004 ∇²min(ζ,0); the right panel reuses the completed ordinary-viscosity decay control.

The added operator preserves mean circulation and dissipates energy and enstrophy. Away from sign interfaces, it increases anticyclonic viscosity to five times the baseline value. Interface fluxes also affect neighboring cyclonic cells; a dominant cyclone must still have compensating anticyclonic circulation elsewhere.

This explicitly imposes a sign preference. It tests the effect of asymmetric dissipation, not whether convection or centrifugal instability supplies that mechanism. Total dissipation differs between cases. No inverse-cascade, convergence or quantitative vortex-selection conclusion is inferred from these movies alone.
