Seeded compressible evolution through t=4000

Compressible total-energy formulation, Ω=3, beta=0, no imposed jet; 256×256×80. Time is nondimensional, not Earth seconds or days. The movies include the earlier completed segments.

Completed through t=4000, with fidelity limitations. Peak Mach exceeded unity and the cap evolved strongly. Completing the integration does not establish a well-controlled, thermally equilibrated regime.

At the last saved state, anticyclonic enstrophy share in depth-mean vorticity filtered to k≤2 is 29.7%, with skewness +1.33. This favors cyclonic squared-vorticity content, not necessarily vortex number. Peak recorded Mach over the displayed history is 1.25. These statistics are distinct from the raw upper-plane measure in the deformation tests.
