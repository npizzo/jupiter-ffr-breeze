Rest-start compressible Ω=5 through t=2000

Compressible total-energy formulation, Ω=5, beta=0, no imposed jet; 256×256×80. Time is nondimensional, not Earth seconds or days. Fixed rotation from t=0; zero initial velocity and the same weak thermal perturbations as the Ω=3 rest-start control. The only physical input changed is Ω.

Completed through t=2000, with fidelity limitations. Peak recorded Mach is 0.505. The mean temperature profile departs by up to 66.1% from its initial state, and the minimum mean cap N² is −0.978 at the endpoint. The cap is no longer wholly stable; thermal equilibrium is not established.

At t=2000, anticyclonic enstrophy share in depth-mean vorticity filtered to k≤2 is 49.9%, with skewness +0.024 and ζ/f extrema −0.039 to +0.041. The share averages 46.6% over t=1000–2000. Thus the large scales are much closer to sign symmetry than the Ω=3 rest-start case at t=2000 (about 32% anticyclonic share); this does not yet establish preferential anticyclone formation. At k≤4 the endpoint share is 49.1%. A dipole containing a persistent anticyclone would be relevant to the Cai–Chan regime even without anticyclonic dominance.
