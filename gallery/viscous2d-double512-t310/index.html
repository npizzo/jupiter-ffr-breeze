Model settings

512 × 512 cells in a periodic 2π × 2π horizontal box; f=1, β=0. Ordinary Laplacian viscosity ν=0.0001, no drag or hyperviscosity. Instability coefficient a=0.0004 in the band 12≤|k|≤16, excluding kx=0. Centered second-order advection and RK3.
