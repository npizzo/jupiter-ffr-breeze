The thermal state is still evolving: maximum relative mean-temperature-profile departure from the original t=0 is 64.5%; minimum cap-mean N² is -0.625. Endpoint peak Mach is 0.479. The compressible-energy proxy is not a conserved anelastic invariant and is not interpreted as numerical energy loss. Completion does not establish thermal equilibrium or LSV saturation.

At the endpoint, the full three-dimensional velocity field has 0.054% of its density-weighted kinetic energy at horizontal wavelengths ≤4 grid cells, and 0.41% at wavelengths ≤8 cells. These energy fractions do not establish vorticity/enstrophy convergence, vertical resolution, or an inverse flux.

Final tracer RMS variations at the three depths are 0.00e+00, 4.04e-10, and 5.79e-08. Near-zero or Float32-scale variations should be read as homogenization, not a visualization failure.
