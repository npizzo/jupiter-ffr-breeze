Partial result and restart

The doubled-forcing run stopped near t=161 when its Courant number reached 0.50007249, just beyond the preset 0.5 guard. This publication ends at the saved restart point t=160; it is not a completed t=400 experiment. The stop is a timestep safety threshold, not evidence by itself of numerical blow-up.

The continuation restarts from the exact t=160 velocity checkpoint with timestep halved from 0.0025 to 0.00125. Forcing, viscosity, grid and all physical terms are unchanged. The restart validation checks evolution to t=162, beyond the earlier stopping point. Restart validation · Continuation settings.

Parameters

512 × 512 cells in a periodic 2π × 2π box; f=1, β=0. Ordinary Laplacian viscosity ν=0.0001; no drag or hyperviscosity. Centered second-order advection and RK3. The forcing is a k²ζ growth term in 12≤|k|≤16, excluding kx=0. Only its coefficient differs between cases. The published 0–160 output uses timestep 0.0025 throughout.

This is prescribed instability forcing in a horizontal 2D model, not explicitly resolved convection. Constant f alone cannot select vorticity sign here. Movies alone do not establish inverse energy transfer or a sign-selection mechanism. Existing control data are reused, with no rerun and no 256² comparison.
