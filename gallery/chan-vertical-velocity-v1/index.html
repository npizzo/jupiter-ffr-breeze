Vertical velocity in rotating convection

Seeded compressible total energy through t=2000, rest-start compressible total energy through t=4000, and anelastic through t=10000. These are the same completed runs shown in the November 26 vorticity gallery; no new simulations or physical changes.

Three columns show z/H = 0.19375, 0.49375, 0.89375. Red is upward velocity; blue is downward. The upper plane samples the originally stable cap (initial interface z/H=0.78), whose thermal state evolves.

Snapshot movies: vertical velocity was saved only in full volumes every 250 time units. Each saved snapshot is held for one second. There is no temporal interpolation, spatial filtering, or framewise normalization. These movies show long-term structural evolution, not resolved plume trajectories. Time and velocity are nondimensional.

Linear color scales use the full sampled extrema, fixed through time and shared across cases at each depth. The three depth limits are ±0.2470, ±0.3351, ±0.3968. Native face velocities are averaged to the adjacent cell centers; endpoint RMS profiles agree with the simulation diagnostics. Grid: 256 × 256 × 80; Ω=3, β=0, no jet.
