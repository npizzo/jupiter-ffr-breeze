# Wider 35°N beta-plane channel: Earth days 0–100

Computed and rendered on Unity. Domain: 6000 × 12000 × 120 km; grid: 256 × 512 × 64. The movie contains 801 actual three-hourly states, with vorticity above passive tracer and three depth columns (19.69, 59.06 and 100.31 km above the bottom). Fixed color mappings across time; no temporal interpolation. The complete MP4 decoded successfully on Unity.

This is the wider-channel Ferrel-cell campaign, separate from the f-plane vorticity-sign experiments. The movie alone does not establish a Ferrel circulation or inverse cascade.

Watch on the [GitHub Pages gallery](https://npizzo.github.io/jupiter-ffr-breeze/#ferrel35-wide100) after publication. `PASS.json` records source metadata hashes and finished-asset checksums; `wide_0_100days.json` records scales and rendering provenance.
