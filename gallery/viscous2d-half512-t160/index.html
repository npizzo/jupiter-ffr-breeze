Half-wavelength forcing through t = 160.

The saved vorticity evolution from t = 0 to 160, with the instability forcing moved to half its original wavelength. Time is dimensionless, not days.

· Open movie

321 actual saved states at 12 frames/s; no interpolation. One horizontal 2D plane, no tracer or depth layers. Positive vorticity is counterclockwise and cyclonic for f > 0. Fixed symmetric asinh colors, width 0.1, span the full extrema over t=0–160 without clipping or framewise rescaling. The poster shows t=160.

Half the forcing wavelength, matched net growth

The forcing band is 24≤|k|≤32, compared with 12≤|k|≤16 in the doubled-forcing reference. The coefficient is a=0.000175, chosen so that (a−ν)k² matches the reference with a=0.0004 and ν=0.0001. Native discrete net growth differs by at most 1.60% on paired modes. Initial velocity fields are unchanged; the initial turbulence remains in band 12–16.

512 × 512 cells in a periodic 2π × 2π horizontal box; f=1, β=0. Ordinary Laplacian viscosity ν=0.0001, no drag or hyperviscosity. Zonal forcing modes kx=0 are excluded. Timestep 0.00125 throughout; centered second-order advection and RK3.

The identical restart fields at t=80 are verified and shown once. This is a frozen publication through t=160, not a claim that the t=400 campaign is complete.

Prescribed instability forcing, rather than explicitly resolved convection. Morphology alone does not establish inverse energy transfer or a mechanism of vortex-sign selection.
