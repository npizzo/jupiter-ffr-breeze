# Ferrel pilot: numerical checks passed, but early vertical motion is underresolved

The four six-hour pilots completed on Unity: heated and unheated turbulent flows, plus their axisymmetric controls. The native momentum-closure, mass-circulation, tracer, cap-stability and restart checks passed. However, the resolution observer found that the emerging vertical motion is concentrated near the horizontal grid scale. These runs do not yet establish resolved convection, persistent Ferrel cells, or an inverse cascade.

The synchronized movies show vorticity and passive tracer at **19.375, 59.375 and 99.375 km above the bottom**, with fixed scales. The heated–unheated comparison is `heating_comparison.mp4`; each case also has its own three-depth movie. All seven saved frames span only **0–6 Earth hours**. This entire interval lies inside the conservatively excluded first 12 hours; 12 hours is not a demonstrated equilibration time.

At 19.375 km and six hours, the heated vertical-velocity spectrum peaks at a wavelength of **47.2 km, only 2.01 grid cells**. After removing the zonal mean, **98.3%** of its variance lies at radial wavelengths shorter than four cells. The unheated run is similar: a 48.1 km peak and 96.7% below four cells. The heated lower-plane absolute heat-cospectral throughput is also concentrated at those short wavelengths. Thus passing conservation and encoding checks is not evidence of convective fidelity. These are small startup disturbances, not a measured physical plume scale.

Heating produces a small emerging vertical response, while the mean-flow response is still almost unchanged. At six hours, heated minus unheated zonal-mean kinetic energy is approximately **0.000001 J/m²**. Both turbulent cases have a six-hour mean centered eddy-to-mean kinetic-energy transfer of approximately **−0.0276 W/m²**. With the stated sign convention, that diagnostic indicates extraction of energy from the mean flow during startup. It does not show eddies feeding jets. This is an approximate covariance diagnostic, with a reported remainder against native advection, rather than a closed kinetic-energy budget. The heated profile's maximum resolved vertical heat flux is only **0.000023 W/m²**, at 5.625 km; the developing unstable layer has not established substantial convective heat transport.

The endpoint mean meridional and vertical velocities are only a few micrometres per second. The axisymmetric controls remain near numerical symmetry. The weak mean circulation in the turbulent cases is therefore measurable, but neither its persistence nor its eddy support has been demonstrated. Larger visible structures or an overturning pattern alone would not resolve that distinction.

The resolution observer passed its analytical and field-consistency checks. Its upper two heat cospectra are nevertheless **indeterminate at the stored temperature precision**: tiny or zero values there must not be interpreted as absent heat transport. Matching Float64 temperature is available at the lowest video plane. Three planes cannot establish bottom-boundary-layer or vertical resolution, and the queued finer grid refines only horizontally.

The cap remains stably stratified. The forcing is a controlled bottom heat input with cap relaxation and global thermal balancing, not a validated Jovian cooling law. In the heated endpoint, cap relaxation supplies about 2.67 W/m² while the global correction removes about 164.67 W/m², balancing the 162 W/m² bottom input. Those terms must remain part of the physical interpretation.

At the latest queue check supplied for this review, the 48-hour unheated case was running and the six-hour horizontal-refinement pilot was pending. Preserve those runs and the queued heated/boundary comparisons, but treat them as diagnostic experiments. Review whether the finer pilot's vertical-velocity peak tracks the grid or remains at a fixed physical wavelength; doubling the horizontal resolution would still give only about four cells across a 47 km feature. Neither outcome alone establishes convergence.

The selected next experiment is a controlled six-hour seed test before another heating amplitude or a long extension: heated and unheated512 cases without thermal noise, plus heated512/1024 cases using identical horizontally band-limited thermal perturbations at fixed physical wavelengths and matched variance. These retain the existing vertical envelope and short vertical correlations; they do not establish vertical convergence. Compare heat-carrying scales, profiles and mean-flow response before deciding whether a longer test is useful. If transport remains concentrated below eight cells, first develop a smaller, more highly resolved convection test with the same vertical stratification and rotation, or explicitly retune the dimensionless regime. Do not infer Jovian fidelity from larger-scale vorticity alone. Any later fine-grid continuation must use its own checkpoint; six-hour agreement cannot validate developed48-hour convection. No additional physical runs have been launched as part of this review.
